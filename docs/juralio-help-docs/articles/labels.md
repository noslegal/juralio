# Labels

Juralio has three types of labels:

1. Risks ~ paired with a red, amber or green tag to signal status of the risk
2. Workflow
3. Hashtags

Risks and workflow labels are controlled at organisation level and matter level.

Organisation admins can turn these off and on and add bespoke labels for the whole organisation.

Matter admins can cater these labels to their specific matter needs and add bespoke labels for only that matter.

Hashtags can be added by anyone within every matter.

## Managing labels

Organisation admins can find labels within the organisation settings of Juralio (above the log out button).

Matter admins can find labels within the matter administration settings (accessed through matter overview or the Juralio switcher)

Within the labels tab you will find standard labels set by Juralio which you can disable if they are not necessary. There is also the option to add and delete bespoke labels for your organisation or matter.

## Hashtag labels

These can be used to make tasks more discoverable when searching within Juralio. Anyone can add them to task details by simply typing the text you wish the label to display.

Hashtag labels can be seen by everyone within the matter.

## Adding labels to a task

Labels are added to tasks in [task details](./task-details.md).

## Finding labels within a matter

### Filtering

Use the filters on the taskmap and roadmap to search for specific labels used within your matters and highlight the tasks they appear in. Select the **detailed view** to see specific labels on the task cards without clicking into task details.

### Label usage

Matter admins can also see what **Risk** and **Workflow** labels have been used and where within the matter administration settings.

## Labels at organisation level

Organisation admins can view and edit the labels that are available for people to use in tasks to identify

* Risks (with traffic light status)
* Workflow nuances

Organisation admins can also add bespoke labels that will be available at organisation level. These can still be disabled at matter level at the discretion of matter admins.
