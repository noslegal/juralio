# Posts: notes and links

Posts (notes and links) have

* a simple workflow - draft or final
* authors - as a record of who wrote the post
* a visibility status - restricted or not. They are not restricted when created - this means that any matter editor can see them. If made restricted, only the authors can see them, or make them non-restricted

## What are posts for?

Posts in Juralio help you record information (e.g. meeting notes, memos) and link to other resources (e.g. documents) in a way which naturally fits within a workstream's structure as defined by its tasks.

This allows the whole team to see what's happening and how it fits in. This reduces the distraction, lack of context, extra work and information overload of more traditional approaches, e.g. email, memos, messaging, and file/attendance notes.

## Types of post

At the moment, there are two types of posts in Juralio - **notes and links.**

Other types of post are in the pipeline such as events and messages.

## Notes

Notes are suitable for

* Capturing a record of a meeting or call - there is a specific post type for this, which includes date/time and attendance fields
* Memos e.g. to summarise research or views taken on an issue
* Recording agreed approaches or even brainstorming

## Links

You already have places to store Word, PDF, emails and other documents. Juralio is not such a place. The primary use of links in Juralio is to allow you to point people to the places where such documents are stored, assuming that this location has a [URL (web address)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/URL).

As well as linking to documents, you can also post a link to anything else that has a URL (web address).

Use either the **Document link** or **Web link** post type to do so.

### Special links

Special links in Juralio allow you to see the link content directly within the Juralio post frame. This lets you work on the content in Juralio without opening a separate web page. This is currently available for the following links:

* Miro
* Google drive: Docs, Sheets and Slides
* Figma

Note: you will only see the content in Juralio if you have permission to see it within the application itself.

## How to post something

You can post a note or a link via the add button (+)
