# Breaking down tasks effectively

Juralio is very flexible in allowing you to break down a task or not depending on context

* use your judgement and common sense to keep tasks a sensible size
* keep things simple, favouring divisions between tasks and levels which are clear and obvious to the matter team
* give some thought to the initial task structure but don't worry too much about the details - you can restructure later depending on how things develop

Some examples are given in [Examples of how to break down tasks](./task-breakdown-examples.md).

Over time, your organisation can use the **templates** feature of Juralio to build up and share know-how on effective ways to structure Tasks.

## Use posts to keep the map readable

The task structure of a matter is a framework for communication and workstream management. Most activity in a matter will is best captured as **posts** (**notes** or **links**) within the task to which they relate.

This allows you to avoid cluttering up the taskmap with too much information. This in turn helps reduce a major risk in complex workstreams - the inability to see the shape of the forest because of all the trees.

If the number of tasks becomes very large on a workstream, other options for decluttering it include

* [splitting](./split-merge-workstreams.md) it into multiple workstreams within the same matter
* marking tasks as not required - this keeps them in the taskmap/ roadmap/tasklist context, but they are filtered out unless you select (via the status filter) to show them
* [archiving tasks](./archiving-and-closing.md) - this is appropriate when you are comfortable that a task will probably not be needed
