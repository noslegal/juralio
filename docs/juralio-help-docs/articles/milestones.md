# Milestones

Milestones are used to represent a significant stage or event in the development of your matter or template.

Milestones are distinct from tasks in the following ways:

* They are represented with a diamond on the milestone card
* They can only be added to your map through the [add button](./creating-things.md) at the bottom left of your screen
* They cannot have subtasks
* They only have one significant date
* They only have two status options: **not yet reached** and **reached**

## Filtering for milestones

Use the filters to highlight milestones on your taskmaps and roadmaps.

We recommend selecting **all tasks** to be shown and then selecting to filter only **milestones**. This will make sure that both reached and not yet reached milestones are highlighted.
