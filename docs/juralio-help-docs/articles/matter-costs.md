# Matter costs

Once your organisation's cost building blocks and rate tables are set up, you can price and track the cost of individual matters.

Only those with cost permissions enabled will be able to see the Matter costs tab. This can be enabled within the matter admin through the People table.

Planning matter-costs looks slightly different depending on whether you are a client or an advisor.
