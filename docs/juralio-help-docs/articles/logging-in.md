# Logging in

1. Open your favourite browser on your computer.
   * Juralio works on recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox
2. Type in the Juralio URL provided by your organisation
   * Save the URL in your browser's favourites/bookmarks
   * Also save it to the taskbar / start menu on Windows / to the dock on Mac
   * Consider also changing your browser settings to open the Juralio URL on start-up
3. Logging in with single sign-on (SSO):
   * Juralio allows SSO with Microsoft and Google authentication
   * If you cannot see these SSO options on your login page, ask your organisation admin to enable this on your account within Juralio organisation settings.
4. Unless SSO is available in your organisation
   * Follow the instructions when you first log in to set up
     1. multi-factor authentication if enabled for your account
     2. a new password
        * use the method approved by your organisation to save your password e.g. password manager
        * Passwords should be minimum 15 characters with complexity applied - see [Password policy](./password-policy.md) for further guidance.
