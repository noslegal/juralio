# My email changed and I cannot log in via SSO

Please note if your organisation uses SSO, the email address change needs to be made on the Juralio side and on the Identity Provider side before an account can use the new credentials to log into Juralio. If this change has not been made prior to the next login you will have issues accessing Juralio.

Get in touch with your admin to clarify the situation. You and your admin may need to contact *{{support_email}}* so that we can change the email address on the existing profile. Please provide the following information:

* Your new email address and your old email address
* CC your company's Juralio Admin and ask them to send a confirmation that we can proceed with the change (required due to security reasons)
