# Matters and workstreams

Juralio allows you to organise your work at two main levels

1. Matters are places for a set of activities undertaken by a particular team for a particular client
2. Workstreams are created within a matter (and belong to only one matter)

You can access them by selecting "Matters" (near the top left of the screen). This will display a matter switcher list. Select the relevant matter to see its workstreams and the people on the matter. If you're a matter admin, you'll also see an option to administer the matter - click that to change the people on the matter, add or close workstreams (or the matter itself).

## Reorganising workstreams

Tasks can be moved between workstreams within the same matter, enabling easy reorganisation as things develop

* Tasks cannot be moved between matters
* This restriction is to avoid inadvertent breaches of confidentiality
* If a set of tasks may be of relevance to future matters, consider using it to create a template

## Matters contain workstreams, which contain tasks

Access in Juralio can controlled

* at matter level
* optionally also at workstream level - by making the workstream 'restricted' to only some of those with access to the matter.

Division of matters into workstreams allows work to be conveniently sub-divided

It's also easy to merge or split workstreams by moving groups of tasks between workstreams within a matter. To protect client confidentiality, you cannot move tasks between matters.

If a group of tasks may be relevant in future matters, the process is to create a template which contains no client confidential information.
