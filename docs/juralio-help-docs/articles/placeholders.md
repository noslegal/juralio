# Placeholders

Placeholders represent individuals who are involved in the matter (e.g. part of a task team or participating in a meeting) but do not have a Juralio account.

Placeholders are visible to everyone with access to the Matter, and will be included in Matter downloads.

## Creating placeholders

**As a matter admin -**

* Go to the People tab within Matter admin
* Select Placeholders
* Create a new placeholder

**As a matter editor -**

* Select to add a new team member to a task
* Start typing the name of the individual you want to represent
* If they do not appear in the list, select the option to create a new placeholder

Once a new placeholder has been added to the matter, they can be seen within the overview and will be available to add to tasks and meeting notes by all editors in the matter.

## Managing placeholders

Matter admins can manage placeholders within the People tab of the matter admin area.

Admins can:

* **Edit names and associated emails**
* **Merge two placeholders**
  * Merging will delete and replace with the preferred placeholder
* **Delete**
  * Deleting placeholders will prevent them from being adding to tasks in the future
  * This won't remove them from tasks they are already associated with as team members or meeting attendees

**Note: any email associated with the placeholder will not be contacted by Juralio, this is for your internal team's information purposes only**
