# Matter and workstream overviews

Overviews provide a high level summary of what's going on in a matter or workstream.

The left panel allows a short description and access to view

1. **Work progress**
   * Within matters, progress of each workstream is given as a roadmap view, each workstream is represented with a bar
   * Within workstreams the same view is given for phase progress but can also be viewed in a taskmap format
2. **People:** a table of who has access to the matters or workstreams and if they are assigned to any tasks
3. **Content:**
   * tables of the tasks and posts within the matter or workstreams
   * resources - a maximum of 15 links that are relevant to your matter or workstream

You can also access matter and workstream downloads from this overview.
