# Deliverables

**Deliverables** are the tangible outputs within a phase. They present similar to a task with a different icon on the taskcards to signal their significance.

Deliverables are distinct from tasks in the following ways:

* They are represented with a square icon on the deliverable card
* They can be added via the taskmap by selecting the "Create as deliverable" and pressing Enter
* They are surfaced during the cost setup and included within cost snapshots as a scope signal

You can also convert a regular task into a deliverable via the options menu on a task.

## Filtering for deliverables

Use the filters to highlight deliverables on your taskmaps and roadmaps.

We recommend selecting **all tasks** to be shown and then selecting to filter only **deliverables.** This will ensure deliverables of all status are surfaced.
