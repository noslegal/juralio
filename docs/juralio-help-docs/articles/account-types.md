# Internal and external accounts

Accounts in Juralio must belong to no more than one organisation. Each organisation has its own matters and templates which cannot be accessed by anyone other than accounts within that organisation.

## Internal and external accounts

Accounts must be internal or external.

Only internal accounts can have the organisations-level roles mentioned below.

Certain abilities (such as moving tasks or archiving items) are restricted to internal accounts.

### External accounts

External accounts allow people outside your organisation to be added to the work in Juralio with limited permissions.

This is useful if working with, for example

* Consultants and experts
* Clients

What **can** an external account do?

* Access matters they are invited to
* Create new tasks and posts in the matter
* Assign dates, team members and labels to tasks
* Follow tasks
* Change task status (excluding not required)

What **restrictions** are placed on external accounts?

External accounts **cannot**:

* Access templates
* Create new matters
* Access the organisation admin
* Access the matter admin
* Move or archive tasks or posts
* See not required tasks or change a task to be not required
* Edit costs even when given costs permissions
