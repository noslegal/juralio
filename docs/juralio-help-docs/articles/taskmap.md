# Taskmap

The taskmap allows you to see how tasks relate to each other.

Each task can have subtasks down to a maximum of six levels from A to F.

Level B tasks are subtasks of phases (level A) and so on.

## Task status

Task colours show their status - not started (light yellow), in progress (yellow) or done (teal).

When a task's status or dates are updated, a change in the status of its parent task or subtasks will be suggested automatically if necessary to keep things consistent.

## Interacting with the taskmap

You can do the following things within the taskmap:

* **Change status:** Select the options menu (three dots) on a task to change its status (e.g. done) and other actions (e.g. moving or copying).
* **Re-order tasks**: Drag a task to reorder it within its sibling group, or move it to a new parent. As moving a task is a significant step, you'll be presented with a deliberate speedbump requiring you to confirm the new move location. You will also be asked to confirm any changes made due to the new logic of the move.
* **Assign team members**: Add a team member to the task if there is not at least one already. If there is one or more, you can hover over the initials to see the name(s).
* **Plan task dates:** Click into a date field to add or edit a date without leaving the taskmap.
* **Create additional tasks:** Hover over a task then press the + button to add siblings (above or below) or a subtask (to the right). Pressing return after entering the new task name will automatically allow you to enter more tasks. Just click away from the task name entry field if you don't wish to do so.
* **Hide and show levels.** Click on to a level indicator (A, B etc) to hide the lower levels. Click again to show them. This is handy if you want to focus attention just on phases on higher level tasks.
* **Hide and show a phase.** Click on the button at the bottom right of a phase card to hide it. Click again to show it. This allows you to focus attention on or away from certain phases.
