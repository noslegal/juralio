# Archiving and closing

The following things in Juralio can be archived or closed.

* Matters and workstreams can be closed and re-opened by matter admins or organisation admins. When closed, nobody can see any of their content.
* Tasks and posts within workstreams can be archived by internal accounts who are matter editors. They cannot be fully deleted. A matter admin can see the archive of tasks and posts within the **Archive** section in the matter admin area. Tasks and posts can be restored to a workstream from this archive.
  * Tasks can also be marked as not required. This keeps them in the taskmap/roadmap/tasklist context (rather than archiving them) but they are filtered out from view unless you choose to show them (via the status filters).
* Only knowledge managers can archive templates and their tasks and posts.
