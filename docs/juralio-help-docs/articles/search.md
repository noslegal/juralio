# Searching for things

Using the search box in the top navigation bar, you can search words appearing in the name or descriptions of:

* Tasks
* Posts
* Workstreams

As you begin typing in the search bar a list of tasks will appear based on your search query. Clicking a result will navigate you to the most relevant place; the task details, body of the post, or the Workstream overview.

If you are within a workstream (taskmap, roadmap or task details) when you search, you will initially only see search results from that workstream, but can choose to "Search everything" if those initial results do not give you what you are seeking.

Clicking the task, post or workstream icons at the top of the results filters out those types of results.
