# Matter-level roles

Standard accounts may have one, multiple or none of these **matter-level roles**

* **Matter admin** - necessary to (1) create / close / reopen workstreams within a matter (2) add matter editors and viewers (3) close a matter. Only internal accounts can be matter admins.
* **Matter editor** - necessary to see and work on workstreams
* **Matter viewer -** necessary to see workstreams but cannot make changes to the work
* **Placeholder** - representation of individuals involved in the work with no access to Juralio

Within a matter's workstreams, a person may have further roles

* **Task team member** - this role is visible to all matter editors to signify who is responsible for a task. Having this role means that you automatically see the relevant task's notifications and task to dos in your inbox. If you want to see notifications and task to dos without being a task team member, you can choose to follow a task.
* **Post author** - this role is visible to all matter editors to signify who wrote the post. By way of exception, if a post is restricted, only its authors can see it.
* **Meeting attendee** - this role is visible to matter editors and viewers that have access to the meeting note, to signify who attended the meeting. If the meeting is restricted, only its authors can see it but anyone can be added as an attendee
