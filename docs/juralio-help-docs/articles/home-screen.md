# Home screen

When you log into your Juralio account you will see the home screen. This contains

* **Recent** - tasks and workstreams you have recently accessed, so you can quickly navigate to them to resume work.
* **To dos -** upcoming tasks you are either a team member of or following, due within the next 30 days.
* **Matter cards gallery** - a high level visualisation of the workload status within each matter. Interact with the cards to go into either the matter overview or its individual workstreams.
* **Tip cards** - useful reminders of things in Juralio, with the option to clear if you'd prefer

Selecting something in the home screen allows you to navigate further into the relevant matter task details or workstream views. Select the Juralio logo (top left of screen) to return to the home screen.

Selecting **Matters** in the top left brings down a switcher that also allows quick navigation to your chosen matter.

Selecting **Templates** in the top left takes you to the template gallery. Here you can access both Juralio [community templates](./templates.md) and templates curated by your organisation.
